ABOUT OPEN ARMS

Where we nurture the future.

Open Arms is a platform that puts verified orphanages, donors, and organisations in the same room — so help actually reaches the children who need it.

How the idea came about

Open Arms started from a simple observation: people want to help orphanages, but rarely know which ones genuinely need it, or what to send. At the same time, smaller orphanages struggle to be visible to donors at all. We built a single place to close that gap.

How we're going to help

Every orphanage on the platform registers with real documentation and reports the number and age range of children in its care. Donors can then give money or specific goods directly toward homes with a verified, current need.

How we build trust

Registration documents are reviewed before an orphanage goes live on the platform. We publish basic statistics about each participating care home so donors can see, in plain terms, where support is going.

Keeping donation centres accountable

We track which centres are active, what they've received, and what they still need — so the same request isn't quietly over-funded while another home goes without.

We do not compromise on India's future.
